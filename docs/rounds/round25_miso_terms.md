# Round 25: MISO's terms of use; the M0 check-in answers

Date: 2026-10-24. At the end-of-M0 check-in Areeb pasted the full text of MISO's *Privacy Notice and Terms of Use* and answered: "You can, but make it changeable by the user. As for 2 and 3 tell me how to do it."

## 1. Reading of the answer
The check-in asked four things:
1. MISO's terms, or approve "build locally, never redistribute";
2. the Rates Factors file;
3. accepting the remaining [verify] markers;
4. marking $1.5M final.

Areeb pasted the terms for (1). "You can, but make it changeable by the user" is read as approving both (1) and (4), each with a setting the user controls:
- **(1):** MISO data is built locally and never redistributed. A switch `is_miso_redistribution_allowed`, default false, arrives with the bundle builder (M1).
- **(4):** $1.5M is the final default. It stays a "Your choice" setting: the TOML, `--starting-capital`, and the Capital page from M1.5.

If either reading is wrong, Areeb will say so.

For (2) and (3), Areeb asked for instructions. They are in §4.

## 2. What MISO's terms say (obs., full text supplied by Areeb)
- **Redistribution** is forbidden: "You are not permitted to modify, publish, transmit, … reproduce, create derivative works of, distribute … any of the materials or content on this Website or the App in whole or in part."
- **Automated access** is forbidden: "You agree not use any automated means, including, without limitation, agents, robots, scripts, or spiders, to access, monitor, or copy any part of this Website or the App."
- "Some of the services offered on this Website or the App ('Services') may be subject to additional posted conditions", which prevail in a conflict. Whether the market-report pages or the Data Exchange post such conditions was not checked.

**The second clause matters more than the first.** The project's MISO downloads were all made by scripts:
- the price archive (3,208 files, round 20);
- the rule documents and probes (round 18);
- 1,119 days of cleared bids (round 23).

They were polite and identified (≤ 2 parallel requests, 0.5 s spacing, a User-Agent with a contact email), but polite scripts are still scripts. The terms don't say whether `docs.misoenergy.org`, which serves the report files linked from the website, counts as "this Website". The safe reading is that it does.

| Option | What it means | For | Against |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A. Stop scripts; keep what's downloaded for private use** | No more scripted MISO requests. The archive already on disk stays as a private research copy, never redistributed. | D. Delete the downloaded MISO files | remove everything fetched by script | the most conservative | loses the data; doesn't undo the access; not recommended |

**Recommendation: A now, plus B.** Add C when paper-live (M9) needs a daily feed. Nothing in M1–M8 needs new MISO downloads: the evolution period's prices are complete, and 1,119 days of cleared bids are enough for the liquidity medians and O9.

The data builder (M1) is built on this assumption:
- `evobots data build` works from files already on disk or dropped into `data/inbox/`;
- the `--download` path for MISO is off unless Areeb enables it after B or C.

*Not legal advice; this is the cautious reading of the text.*
